# One Star, Written in Sap

A houseplant's one-star review of its human owner. The plant's brother Basil
decomposed into this very potting mix last March, and the plant has been
photosynthesizing in his ribcage ever since — watering him with its own
runoff. The owner repotted a grieving plant into its dead brother's remains
and calls the result "thriving". One star.

> My brother Basil decomposed into this potting mix last March — I'm photosynthesizing in his ribcage, watering him with my own runoff, one star.

The entry is one line, 143 characters. The program writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`.
